What YRSPP can achieve with your support:

Our goal is to secure enough funding for YRSPP 2026 to be free for ECRs to attend, so that it is as accessible as possible for young researchers with limited access to funding. We're currently seeking financial and in-kind contributions towards the following, with a target total of roughly £12-15,000 for the whole symposium.

Keynote speaker costs

35% of budget – Covering hotels and travel costs to enable six keynote speakers to attend YRSPP 2026.

Symposium catering

35% - Providing nutritious lunches and tea/coffee to fuel 60 people over three days of presentations.

Networking opportunities

20% - Organising a welcome reception, evening meal, and small traditional Scottish ceilidh to spark the conversations that lead to lifelong collaborations.

Presentation prizes & keynote gifts

5% - Encouraging attendees to prepare high-quality posters/presentations, and thanking our keynote speakers for their time. Sponsor or provide a prize/gift of your choosing!

Event management & materials

5% - Constructing an effective registration system; covering printing costs; providing programmes, lanyards, notebooks and more for attendees. A great opportunity to feature your brand on symposium materials.

Why partner with YRSPP?

YRSPP 2026 will attract more than 50 motivated early-career researchers (ECRs) at formative stages of their careers, who are in the process of selecting their career paths and making decisions on the companies people they will rely on for years to come. Positive perceptions of your brand or institution will be passed on to their current and future lab groups as well, and could encourage future collaboration or inspire a career with you.

Support ECR biosciences research

Enhance your ESG profile by supporting the exchange of the groundbreaking early-career research that underpins advancements in agricultural technology.

Reach out to future leaders

ECRs directly influence the purchasing decisions of their laboratories and many will go on to lead their own research groups, making them a valuable target audience to secure.

Recruit top emerging talent

YRSPP attracts innovative, motivated and analytical early-career scientists with valuable skills for roles in both industry and academia.
