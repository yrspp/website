Presenting a talk or poster at YRSPP

All ECR delegates are strongly encouraged to apply to present a poster and/or give a talk at YRSPP 2026. This is a great opportunity to present your work to a supportive audience and receive constructive feedback from peers and senior researchers. This year we're also introducing the option of a flash talk for anyone bringing a poster – this is a 2-3 minute 'elevator pitch' introducing your subject to everyone so they can come and visit your poster.

Last year, roughly 70% of YRSPP attendees presented their research, leading to interesting discussions and useful feedback.

To protect and encourage sharing of unpublished work, photography/recording of posters or presentation material will be prohibited.

What can I present?

Standard talk: 15-20 minutes + 5 minutes for questions.

  • Presentations should be prepared using Microsoft Powerpoint or exported as a PDF – we are unable to open Apple Keynote files on the venue computers.
  • Exact presentation length will be confirmed once we have received all applications and established the number of presenters.
  • Try to capture the audience’s attention with large figures and images, keep text to a minimum and consider what the key takeaways are on each slide.

Poster: A0 (1190mm x 840 mm)

  • Posters should be in portrait orientation.
  • Posters will be left on display for the full duration of the symposium, to enable viewing and discussions during lunch/refreshment breaks as well as the formal poster sessions.
  • Ensure text is large and legible, and place the focus on your figures and diagrams.

Flash talk: 1-2 minutes (no question time)

  • Available to delegates presenting a poster who are not giving a standard talk.
  • Exact presentation length will be confirmed once we have received all applications and established the number of presenters.
  • Focus on giving a clear and concise overview of your topic and grabbing the audience’s interest so they come and see your poster!
How do I apply to present at YRSPP?

An abstract submission form will be emailed to you after you register to attend YRSPP 2026. This will require you to indicate what you'd like to present (i.e. poster, talk, flash talk) and provide a written abstract (<200 words) about your work. You'll also have the option to send in a graphical abstract to accompany your written one. Abstracts will be published in the YRSPP 2026 abstract book.

We'll use the abstracts to select and schedule presentations for the symposium. We aim to provide every delegate with at least one opportunity to present (though this will depend on numbers), so please do apply to present your research – if competition is very high we'll be in touch.

When will I hear back about my application to present?

We will be in touch with details about your presentation/poster application after the registration window closes.

Are there prizes?

Yes, there will be prizes to encourage and recognise excellence in accessible, engaging science communication. These will include certificates, multi-retailer gift cards, and an antibody of the winner's choice from Agrisera.

Cancellation policy

If you need to cancel your registration for any reason, we may be able to arrange a partial refund up until the closure of registration. Please email yrspp.mail@gmail.com to do so. After the close of registration, fees paid are non-refundable.
